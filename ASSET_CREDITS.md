# Asset credits

This independent research exhibition uses public cat photographs and limited crops of the actual generated flashcard UI as research evidence.

Photographs were resized for web delivery. Evidence images were cropped to the card area only, without changing their content. Hero cards are explicitly labeled artistic re-creations, not screenshots of generated cards.
